About the museum

The Philippines and Japan have traded, travelled and traded stories for more than a thousand years. Hiraya gathers their artifacts in one place so you can see the links for yourself.

1. Choose a gallery

Start with the Philippines, Japan, or a theme such as burial, writing or craft.

2. Look closely

Rotate each artifact, zoom in on the details and read the curator's notes.

3. Listen and compare

Play the audio story, then set two artifacts side by side.

Questions

Is the app free?

Which languages are supported?

English, Filipino and Japanese.

Can I use it offline?

You can download a gallery before a trip and view it without a connection.

Where do the artifacts come from?

They come from partner museums and collections in both countries, each credited on its artifact page.
